Privacy policy

Bionic Page is a local reading aid. It runs entirely in your browser and does not operate any server.

What the extension collects

Nothing is transmitted. The extension makes no network requests, contains no analytics and no telemetry, and has no accounts or sign-in.

What the extension stores

Only your own settings: the enable switch, the selected mode, intensity, minimum word length, appearance options, and any per-site rules you add. They are stored with your browser's extension storage, which may sync through your browser vendor's own account if you have sync enabled. They never pass through us.

Why it asks for access to all sites

The extension has to read the text of the page you are on to emphasize it. The <all_urls> host permission is required because a reading aid is only useful if it works on the pages you choose. Page content is processed in memory and discarded when you leave the page; nothing is uploaded or saved.

Permissions in plain words

  • storage — save your settings.
  • activeTab — affect the tab you click the icon on.
  • <all_urls> — read and restyle page text locally.

Revoking access

Turning the extension off, disabling it, or removing it stops all processing immediately. Removing the extension deletes its stored settings.
