Turn any web page into a bionic reading page.

    04 How it works

    One reversible pass, three moves

    No servers, no accounts, no permanent changes. The extension reads your page locally and puts every word back exactly as it found it.

    01

    Scan

    Walks the visible text nodes of the page, skipping scripts, inputs, code, and anything marked off-limits.

    02

    Anchor

    For every word, the chosen mode bolds its leading letters — a predictable fixation point your eye can land on.

    03

    Restore

    Toggle off and the original text nodes come back byte-for-byte. A test literally diffs the DOM before and after.

    05 Controls

    Three ways to switch it, one rulebook per site

    A shortcut for your hands, a popup for setup, a floating control for the page — and per-site rules so long-form sites get anchoring while everything else stays untouched.

    Command it

    One keystroke toggles the current page. Everything else is a click away.

    Ctrl+ Shift+ Y
    • Shortcut — toggle the page under your cursor, no mouse needed.
    • Popup — pick a mode, set intensity, open the options page.
    • Floating control — a quiet pill on the page for instant tweaks.

    Per-site rules

    On for the sites you read, off everywhere else. Try the switches — they work.

    W en.wikipedia.org half · 0.50
    D developer.mozilla.org classic · 0.40
    N news.ycombinator.com vowel
    G github.com off

    06 Safety

    Safe by construction

    The rewrite is a local pass over text nodes, gated so it cannot touch the parts of a page that must not change.

    What it never touches

    • script, style, code, and pre
    • Form fields, contenteditable, and math
    • SVG, navigation, buttons, and toolbar or menu roles
    • Anything marked data-bionic="off" or aria-hidden="true"

    It also skips URLs, email addresses, and long unbroken runs.

    What it does handle

    • Single-page apps and infinite scroll, through a MutationObserver
    • Text a framework replaces inside an existing paragraph
    • Text inside open shadow roots (web components)

    Turning it off restores the original text nodes exactly; a test compares innerHTML before and after.

    08 Privacy

    No network. No analytics. No accounts.

    Bionic Page makes no network requests and has no analytics, accounts, or telemetry. Your settings are the only thing stored, and they stay in the browser's own storage. The rewrite runs entirely on your machine; the <all_urls> host permission exists so page text can be read locally, and nothing is ever uploaded. Removing the extension removes the settings.

That is exactly why Bionic Page is framed as configurable visual anchoring — a comfortable, predictable fixation point — rather than a speed-reading promise.
