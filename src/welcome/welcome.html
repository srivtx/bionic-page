Bionic Page

You're set up.

Bionic Page will emphasize the leading part of each word on the pages you read. It works automatically; you can change everything from the popup.

  1. 1 Open any article. Click the toolbar icon to see the status and controls.
  2. 2 Pick a mode. Start with Half, then try Classic, Vowel anchor, or Low distraction.
  3. 3 Toggle fast. Press Ctrl Shift Y (or ⌘ Shift Y) or use the floating Bp control.

No network requests, no analytics, no accounts. Your settings stay in this browser. Code blocks, editors, form fields, and math are never touched, and turning it off restores the page exactly.
